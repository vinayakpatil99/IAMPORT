How an identity moves
Pick a stage to see what the automation does.







Pre-hire and hire
A new record arrives from the HR system before the start date. Saviynt creates the identity, provisions the Active Directory account in the correct OU, and assigns the groups the role needs from day one.


Update and transfer
When a department, location or manager changes, mover and technical rules detect it and adjust attributes and group access automatically, removing what no longer applies.


Disable and rehire
On exit, the account is disabled with the right UAC handling and access is removed. If the person returns, the rehire flow restores a clean account instead of creating a duplicate.
